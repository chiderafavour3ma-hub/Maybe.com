Our Story

Born from fire.
Raised by flavour.

From a two-pot roadside dream in Surulere to Lagos’ most celebrated modern Nigerian kitchen.

Chapter One — 2016

It started with Mama’s pot.

“If the stew doesn’t make you close your eyes, start again.”

Our founder, Chef Adéola Bakare, grew up grinding pepper by hand and tending firewood under her grandmother’s watchful eye. ÁJÉ began as a Sunday-only kitchen serving 20 plates. Today we serve thousands — but every pot still gets grandmother-level attention.
